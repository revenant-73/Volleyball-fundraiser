# Before connecting live services

Milestone one is a local and hosted review prototype, with simulated payments/email only. This list gates live fundraising, not review hosting. It does not claim that legal, school, provider, payment, or authorization requirements have been verified. The hosted owner password and transactional snapshot support review; they do not complete production administrator provisioning, media, ledger/outbox, retention or recovery requirements.

## Proposed first player launch for TVVC

Start with a small group of consenting families, one campaign and one payment method. Families share their own player links. In this release, the application would keep donor contact uploads and fundraising email disabled. Launch Venmo and automated email separately after verifying their respective reconciliation and permission requirements.

1. In a Stripe test environment, trace a contribution from checkout through server-side confirmation, actual fee data, a single reconciled dues credit and a refunded or disputed payment. Repeat payment events and try concurrent postings. Start with a manual, administrator-reviewed posting to the club's dues ledger.
2. Rehearse player setup, guardian authorization, approved public profiles, withdrawal of permission and emergency campaign closure. Check the links and QR codes on physical phones, including Safari.
3. Approve the live financial rules, disclosures, consent, retention and recovery procedures. Record who monitors payments, resolves exceptions and can pause collection. Verify a current recovery file and assign a custodian for credential renewal.
4. Obtain explicit approval to open the named campaign to the selected families. Expand after comparing settled payments, actual fees, posted dues credits and any refunds with the club's records.

The [local Stripe rehearsal](Stripe_Sandbox.md) covers signed confirmation, actual fees, duplicate delivery, refunds, failed and expired checkouts, disputes and settlement after a stop. Dispute outcomes and delayed bank settlement still need a provider sandbox with the applicable permissions and payment methods. Live configuration and launch approval remain open.

## Girls Club family dues purpose and policy approval

The TVVC Girls Club fundraiser is an optional way for individual players and families to cover season dues. Team/club campaigns organize these efforts and show aggregate results. TVVC receives the money; each player's intended benefit must be tracked through a separate dues-credit process. The owner approved the defaults below on 2026-10-17 for fictional implementation/rehearsal only. Real collection, real dues postings and changes to season agreements remain closed. See [Project Brief: TVVC dues-credit defaults](../Project_Brief.md#proposed-tvvc-dues-credit-defaults) for the full policy and example.

- Approve eligible season charges, the season/account mapping, who may view each family's finances, administrator responsibilities and the authoritative source for balances. Set each player's target against eligible outstanding dues; keep full balances, invoices and payment plans private.
- **Dues credits:** recommend crediting only confirmed receipts less verified actual processing fees, capped at eligible outstanding dues at posting time. Keep eligible credit separate from applied credit. An administrator records a posting reference only after the credit is reconciled with the dues system. Pending, rejected, refunded or disputed money cannot generate new available credit.
- **Processing fees:** recommend deducting actual transaction processing fees only, with no platform percentage or automatic hosting/email deductions. Unknown fees leave credit awaiting reconciliation; a verified fee-free payment records zero explicitly. Start without a donor surcharge or automatic fee gross-up. TVVC absorbs non-returned processing/refund costs by default rather than silently changing the donor refund or family obligation. Stripe notes that original processing fees are not returned on refunds; verify the actual account's fee schedule before launch ([Stripe refund-fee guidance](https://support.stripe.com/questions/understanding-fees-for-refunded-payments)).
- **Excess funds:** recommend stopping new player-directed payments and future player solicitation once the reconciled target is covered. Hold any overage unapplied and default to refunding the excess to the original donor. A different use requires recorded affirmative donor agreement and owner review. No automatic teammate redistribution, next-season carryover, or family cash payout. Permit clearly disclosed general club support as a separate donor choice.
- **Withdrawals/cancellation:** recommend promptly stopping that player's fundraising and new credit postings, then calculating eligible dues still owed under the approved season agreement. Apply/retain contributions only against those obligations; reverse excess credits and return unused amounts to original donors. Keep ambiguous cases on owner hold; do not automatically forfeit funds or pay the family. Distinguish withdrawal from the team from withdrawal of photo/publication permission, which requires prompt visibility action without automatically deciding the financial outcome.
- Approve a refund/dispute procedure with partial refunds, multiple donors, refund ordering, unavailable payment-method refunds, donor nonresponse, retained fees and exceptions. Recommended allocation: reverse/refund the most recent unapplied contributions first, then the most recent applied contributions if needed; preserve each donor's payment link and never refund more than that donor's remaining refundable amount. Cases the provider cannot refund stay recorded for owner resolution; silence is not consent to redirect funds.
- Approve versioned donor/family disclosures before payment: recipient organization, beneficiary/season, fee treatment, credit timing, excess rules, withdrawal rules and contact for questions. Record the accepted policy version with the contribution. TVVC acknowledgement wording must not claim charitable tax deductibility. Century requires separate school approval of any family-credit arrangement.

## Dues-credit implementation and acceptance gate

The fictional dues workflow now tracks private balances, family payments/corrections, reconciled credit references, fee holds, excess redirection evidence, partial/full simulated refunds, disputes, withdrawal obligations and external reversal reconciliation. Independent eight-hour read-only family summary links can be issued/revoked by the owner. See [Family Dues Workflow](Family_Dues_Workflow.md). There is no connection to TVVC billing, actual refund execution or live payment/email adapter. The checks below still gate real use; passing a fictional rehearsal does not clear them.

- Implement season-specific family account authorization and private balances independently of public athlete pages and private athlete setup links. A setup-link holder must not gain access to family invoices or balances. Show gross contributions, known fees, pending payments, credit awaiting reconciliation, applied credits, excess and remaining dues distinctly, with the balance's as-of time.
- Add auditable, integer-cent credit allocations/postings/reversals linked to original contributions and season accounts. Preserve donation status, original donor intent and ledger history. Enforce transactionally that donation net funds and eligible dues are not allocated twice; unknown fees cannot become zero by default. Support partial refund/dispute amounts rather than relying on the prototype's full-refund status alone.
- Start with reviewed export/manual posting if that is sufficient. Record stable batch/line IDs and external posting references; retrying an export/import or administrator action must not apply credit twice. Reconcile family direct payments and credits before marking a posting applied. No billing integration, payment-plan changes or separate TVVC website changes are authorized by this checklist.
- Implement owner-reviewed refund/redirection and withdrawal cases, with evidence, unresolved holds, credit reversals and resulting balance review. Freeze affected available credit for an open dispute; only verified resolution releases it. Reversing a credit must not silently charge the family or rewrite its season agreement.
- Label public progress as confirmed gross raised or reconciled net credit, according to the chosen measure; never call gross contributions “dues paid.” Team/club rollups count each contribution once, not again when posted as credit. Do not expose remaining family dues publicly. Reconcile and halt player-directed payments/solicitations at target completion, with safe handling of payments already in flight; other players remain eligible. Shared-contact deduplication and indefinite pending-payment holds must survive player completion/withdrawal.
- Rehearse fictional scenarios: known/unknown/zero fees; confirmation and repeat webhooks; simultaneous contributions or admins; family direct payment racing a credit; duplicate posting/import; overage; partial/full refunds and retained fees; dispute won/lost; withdrawal before/after credit; season cancellation; donor consent/refusal/nonresponse; and unauthorized balance access. Verify public totals, family balances, audit entries and actual external posting evidence agree before live approval.
- Record owner approval of the financial policy and confirm its fit with TVVC's season agreement, accounting treatment and payment-provider requirements. Rehearse with fictional data before separately authorizing real family/donor collection and live payments/email.

## Email/provider/permission gate

- Obtain written confirmation from the chosen email provider that the specific **athlete-uploaded contact fundraising** workflow is permitted. No provider has been selected or approved here.
- Establish the applicable recipient permission requirements, collection evidence, sender identification, unsubscribe handling, complaints, bounce handling, retention, deletion, and applicable school/organization obligations. Athlete entry of an email address must not be treated as proof of recipient consent.
- Approve organization-sent templates and a separate transactional thank-you policy. Configure verified sending domains, sender identity, authenticated provider events, delivery monitoring, and opt-out suppression persistence. Unsubscribe should not require an account.
- Implement test-recipient allowlisting and a separate production send gate. Verify no real recipients can be contacted during rehearsals. Confirm whether transactional acknowledgements remain permitted for unsubscribed contacts; hard bounce/complaint suppression must prevent further delivery.

## Athlete/guardian authorization process

1. Before collection/publication, give athlete and guardian the exact proposed public display name, photo/message usage, fundraising purpose, publication period, and withdrawal contact.
2. Record affirmative athlete/guardian authorization by the school/club’s approved process. Store a dated record/evidence reference, who reviewed it, scope of permitted use, and any expiry/withdrawal. Do not assume the prototype checkbox alone meets this process.
3. Administrator reviews the actual submitted content and records the authorization reference before approval. Editing the public content requires another review; the prototype invalidates approval automatically.
4. Document withdrawal, unpublishing, deletion/retention and access rules, including how archived emails/exports/backups are handled. Avoid collecting unnecessary birth dates, full legal names, addresses or other public minor information.

This proposed workflow must be approved by the relevant organization; it is not a verified authorization/legal policy.
