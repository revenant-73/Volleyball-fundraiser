# Family season dues workflow

The owner approved the financial defaults on 2026-10-17 for fictional implementation. This workflow helps individual families cover eligible Girls Club season dues. Team/club campaign totals summarize fundraising; gross raised and applied dues credit are separate measures. All amounts are integer cents internally. Payments, refunds, billing changes and email remain simulated or blocked.

## Rehearse

1. Sign into the TVVC workspace and open **Dues**. In the pilot, an owner operates the rehearsal; other administrators can review summaries. Choose **Create fictional dues rehearsal**. Each click creates a separate draft; it never resets an existing campaign. Repeating the same network request returns the same campaign.
2. Select Sample Riley or Sample Morgan. Each starts with fictional $1,000 season charges/target, a $100 confirmed contribution with a $3 fee, a $50 confirmed contribution with unknown fees and a $25 pending contribution. Only $97 is initially available for posting. No invitations or donor messages are queued by generating this data.
3. Choose **Record reconciled credit posting**, select the $100 source, enter $60 and a unique fictional external posting reference, and add an audit note. Applied credit becomes $60 and remaining dues $940. In a real manual process the reference would be entered only after verifying the posting in the dues system; this rehearsal changes no external system.
4. Reconcile the unknown fee with **Reconcile fictional contribution fee**; zero must be entered explicitly if verified. Confirm the fictional pending source through its dedicated rehearsal action, entering its verified fee. Pilot payment/reconciliation routes remain closed; these actions accept only generated fictional source records.
5. Record a fictional direct family payment, or reverse a mistaken payment using the original entry and a new external reference. Changes to charges/payments can reduce available dues. Credit exceeding the updated balance is reversed newest posting first and flagged for external reconciliation; no automatic family charge occurs.
6. Review excess in the balance cards. **Simulate donor refund** records an amount and mock refund reference against its original contribution. It supports partial and full refunds and keeps original fees recorded. **Redirect excess with donor consent** requires explicit consent evidence and only accepts unapplied excess; it does not increase campaign fundraising totals again or move that money to another family. No silent carryover or cash payout exists.
7. Choose **Withdraw family / cancel season** and enter the total eligible season obligation after reviewing the fictional agreement. This is the total charge obligation, not an additional charge or remaining-balance adjustment. Fundraising stops, the family link is revoked, and credit above the obligation is reversed. Use the explicit **Apply credit to reviewed withdrawal obligation** action if unposted confirmed funds must cover an obligation. Refund unused sources separately; no refund is executed automatically.
8. Reconcile each outstanding external reversal with its real-world-equivalent fictional reference using **Reconcile external credit reversal**. New postings stay blocked until reversal reconciliation is complete. The original posting, reversal and reconciliation receipt all remain in history.
9. Issue a private family summary link only after recording the guardian verification note. It is read-only, account-scoped and expires after eight real hours; replacement, revocation and withdrawal invalidate the old link. Deliver through an established verified channel, never a donor/public page. No email is sent. Athlete setup links cannot read family balances. Treat the family link as a sensitive bearer capability; it is not a guardian identity-verification system.
10. Download posting history CSV. Rows include stable event IDs, player/season, type, source donation ID, signed integer-cent amount, external reference, timestamp, actor and note. A reconciliation receipt is distinct from a credit entry; do not sum every row as a new credit or re-import rows without stable-ID deduplication. There is no automatic billing import.

## Financial behavior

- Unknown fees and pending payments provide no available credit. Credit is capped by both confirmed net funds for the selected source and the current outstanding eligible dues. Donation gross is never increased by a dues posting.
- Full refunds remove principal from gross totals but retain known processor costs; known net can be negative. Partial refunds/lost disputes appear in donor receipts and the donation ledger, and exports preserve original principal separately from cumulative refunded/disputed cents. Family summaries separately show gross awaiting fee reconciliation and funds redirected to general club support.
- Posting requests have durable replay IDs, account versions and unique external references. Lost-response retries return the original result; changed payloads conflict. Concurrent stale actions fail for review rather than applying twice. Database transactions commit the account, donation changes and replay evidence together.
- Open disputes hold affected unposted funds without silently removing an already posted credit. A lost dispute or refund reverses affected credit once; a won dispute releases the hold. Refunded donor funds do not regain solicitation eligibility. Refund/dispute resolution and retained fees require owner review; nothing is charged to families automatically.
- Fundraising stops at a reconciled target or withdrawal. It does not restart automatically after a refund or correction. An active account with outstanding dues and reconciled reversals can be reopened explicitly after owner review. Shared-contact deduplication and indefinite pending-payment holds remain in force for other player associations.
- Refund selection and ordering remain manual owner decisions: use the approved newest-unapplied-first rule, then newest applied contributions when required. The app never claims to have returned money. To refund a source with redirected funds, first use **Reverse redirection before donor refund** with evidence of the reviewed disposition. This preserves the original redirection and its reversal; no automatic consent reversal or payment-provider fallback exists.

## Storage and boundaries

Account metadata and append-only events are stored in each athlete's existing per-record data. Existing local atomic snapshots, hosted review Postgres transactions and serialized pilot Turso transactions persist them without a schema migration or data reset. Pilot persistence rejects removal/rewrite of existing dues events. Donation refund/dispute/redirection fields use the existing donation records and ledger audit. No existing campaign receives accounts automatically; review TVVC administrators may explicitly set up accounts for fictional athletes. Century credits remain blocked pending separate school approval.

Backups include dues accounts/history and donation adjustments. Family-summary access hashes are cleared in exported recovery snapshots so restore cannot resurrect those links. The existing recovery key and media/backup retention behavior are unchanged.

The pilot exposes dues writes only to an active owner and only in generated fictional rehearsal campaigns. Its public campaign pages, athlete onboarding, payment events, ledger reconciliation routes and email workers remain closed. An independent private family summary route is available for fictional review; it exposes balances and the approved display name, not donors, ledger details, account history or editing capabilities. Application activity logs omit private links and donor details.

One account is scoped to one player record/campaign and season; cross-campaign/sibling account consolidation is not implemented. No TVVC website integration, live collection or external account change is authorized by this rehearsal.
